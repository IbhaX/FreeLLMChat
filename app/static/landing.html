Capabilities

Built like a product,
not a chat window.

Four systems working together - routing, rendering, research, and agents - so answers arrive structured, cited, and fast.

01

Every provider, one pool

Groq, OpenRouter, NVIDIA, Cloudflare, AI Horde, Ollama and more - keyed, rotated, rate-limited and failed over automatically. One request fans out across 30+ providers; the fastest healthy model answers.

02

Rich structured answers

Replies render as dossiers, carousels, tables, charts and galleries - enforced by the nova-response protocol. Ask "who is X" and get a sourced profile card, not a wall of markdown.

03

Deep research

Bounded multi-stage pipelines with live progress, sources, citations and a shareable final report.

04

Agents & roleplay

21 built-in expert agents, custom personas, characters, and long-term memory across every conversation.

05

Yours, end to end

Accounts, role-based access, and per-user history. Keys and settings stay behind the admin role.

Why IbhaX

One workspace for
every conversation.

Stop juggling provider dashboards, keys, and formats. IbhaX is the single surface where chat, research, and roleplay share one conversation model - and one protocol.

01

Never down

A provider hiccups, the router quietly retries the next best model. You see the answer, not the error.

02

Never raw

Responses are validated and repaired into components - cards, charts, dossiers - before they reach the screen.

03

Never forgets

Vector memory follows you across chat, research, and roleplay. Context compounds instead of resetting.

04

Never locked in

Local Ollama models sit next to frontier APIs. Swap providers mid-run from the progress card.

03
